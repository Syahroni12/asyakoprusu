About Us

Asya Koprusu consulting firm is operating in Turkiye as an extension of MITRA & Wahana Group who has 16 years of experience, recruiting more than 30.000 labors to employers within 9 branch offices all over the world. MITRA & Wahana Group has been accredited by several goverment institutions as one of the best companies in its sector. Our aim is to carry out this experience to Asya Koprusu consulting to build trusted relationship with our partners in Turkiye and give them high quality services with empowered manpower to improve the human quality and welfare of the young generation. With our strategic and strong partner MITRA & Wahana Group, we are happy to provide services in a wide range of areas, focusing on many different sectors, from Tourism to Textile Industry, from Construction Industry to certified SPA therapists.

Following are the service that we, Asya Koprusu consulting are able to provide:

  • To provide competent caregivers for all kinds of elderly, children and people in need of care or to act as an intermediary in their transition to another job.
  • Providing care services for the elderly and physically disabled people without providing shelter.
  • Providing outsourced nursing services outside of hospitals.
  • To provide or mediate caregivers for those looking for caregivers on daily, monthly or longer-term contracts.
  • Providing consultancy regarding the provision of help to the families of those in need of care.
  • Providing professional services in many areas, from customer relations to drivers, from gardeners to cleaners, from hotel staff to cooks and from assistants to certified spa therapists.
